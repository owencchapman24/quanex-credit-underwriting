# Tyman acquisition comparability map

The August 1, 2024 Tyman acquisition is the principal break in the historical
series. The datasets encode the following boundaries rather than manufacturing
a continuous combined-company history.

| Period or layer | What it represents | Permitted analytical use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2021-FY2023 reported | Legacy Quanex; FY2023 includes LMI from November 1, 2022 | Reported historical trend | No Tyman operations |
| FY2024 reported income/cash flow | Legacy Quanex plus three months of Tyman after August 1, 2024 | Reported results only | Mixed perimeter; not comparable to FY2023 or full FY2025 without analysis |
| October 31, 2024 reported balance sheet | Post-acquisition consolidated balance sheet | Reported opening point for FY2025 | Includes acquisition financing and preliminary purchase accounting |
| FY2025 reported | Full year of the acquired operations | Reported current year | Includes integration, purchase accounting, restructuring, recall, segment change, and impairment effects |
| SRC-008 company pro forma | Unaudited combined FY2023, nine months to July 31, 2024, and July 31, 2024 balance sheet | Separate reference for acquisition comparability | Preliminary, assumption-dependent, not indicative, excludes synergies and integration costs |
| SRC-012 pro forma summary | Unaudited Tyman/LMI pro forma revenue and net income for FY2022-FY2024 | Separate summary reference | Limited metrics; FY2023 net income is $1 thousand above SRC-008 and is retained as a separate source variant |
| Future analyst adjustments | Not created in Phase 1 | Phase 2 only | Must be explicit, sourced, and must never overwrite reported or pro forma facts |

The company reorganized its reporting in FY2025 into Hardware Solutions,
Extruded Solutions, and Custom Solutions and recast FY2024/FY2023 segment tables.
Those recasts change segment presentation but do not convert pre-August 2024
consolidated results into historical combined Quanex/Tyman results.

Goodwill, intangibles, depreciation/amortization, interest, taxes, and working
capital are especially affected by acquisition accounting and financing. Phase 2
should not infer run-rate economics by mechanically bridging the FY2024 reported
period to FY2025 or by mixing the pro forma balance sheet with reported cash-flow
facts.

The FY2025 10-K final purchase-price allocation includes measurement-period
changes to inventory, prepaid/other assets, property and equipment, goodwill,
accounts payable, and deferred taxes versus the preliminary FY2024 allocation
(SRC-001, Note 2). Phase 2 must use the final allocation where an acquisition
accounting detail matters and must not treat the preliminary opening allocation
as silently final.
