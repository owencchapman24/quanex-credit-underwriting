# Limitations

This portfolio project is public-information underwriting with a December 15, 2025 information cutoff and a hypothetical January 31, 2026 closing. It does not use management projections, lender materials, private diligence, post-cutoff analytical evidence, or an official covenant-compliance certificate.

Final debt-document definitions and closing documentation remain unresolved. Pricing, fees, hedging, syndication, collateral, guarantees, perfection, priority, accessible cash, and closing coverage require diligence. The selected structure also depends on a conditional $15 million non-debt source that may not be replaced with additional debt.

FY2024 contains only approximately three months of Tyman and FY2025 is the first full post-Tyman year. Company pro forma information is not reported history. The $302.284 million goodwill impairment is mathematically reversed in lender-base EBITDA but remains a major adverse acquisition and forecasting signal. Scenario outcomes depend on documented operating, timing, seasonality, working-capital, distribution, and mitigation assumptions; they are not management forecasts. Severe cases exhaust liquidity and fail mandatory payments.

Every financing path retains material refinancing dependency. The selected path's $324.780 million ultimate bank-debt gap is a point-in-time January 31, 2031 result and cannot be compared without qualification to the existing facility's August 1, 2029 gap. The July 31, 2029 common-horizon comparison is unfavorable to the selected structure.

Official facility recovery remains `N/D`. Going-concern and asset-realization cases are separate illustrative alternatives and are not appraisals, additive values, legal opinions, or evidence of lender access. Recovery does not improve the borrower default-risk assessment. The provisional `Elevated` assessment is qualitative and project-specific, with no bank-grade, agency-rating, or probability-of-default mapping.

The workbook was tested with the recorded Windows, Python, Node, artifact-tool, LibreOffice, and Excel versions. Other environments are untested. These spreadsheet-engine compatibility boundaries are part of the conclusion. LibreOffice and Excel can rewrite XLSX package metadata; the normalized semantic fingerprint, structure, formulas, values, formats, charts, validations, names, calculation settings, scenario captures, and saved scenario govern reproducibility rather than raw ZIP identity alone.
